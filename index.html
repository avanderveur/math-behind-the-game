Calculus Intelligence Layers

This Project Turns Baseball Metrics Into Motion Analysis

Each layer of the system connects a baseball term to a calculus idea. The goal is not just to calculate numbers, but to explain what those numbers mean in the game.

01

Pitch Velocity

A pitch is position changing over time. The derivative s′(t) measures how fast the ball moves toward home plate.

02

Reaction Time

Time to the plate is determined by distance divided by velocity. Faster pitches create smaller decision windows.

03

Spray Direction

The ball does not only travel forward. Its horizontal direction across the field changes the landing zone and defensive value.

04

Maximum Height

The highest point of a hit occurs when the vertical derivative changes from positive to negative.

Project Thesis

Baseball Analytics Are Motion Analysis

Baseball is a game of moving objects. A pitch changes position on its way to the plate. A batted ball changes position as it rises, reaches a maximum height, and falls. Differential calculus explains that motion by measuring how position changes over time.

Derivative

Velocity is the derivative of position, which makes pitch speed and batted ball speed calculus concepts.

Second Derivative

Acceleration explains how velocity changes, especially the downward effect of gravity on a hit baseball.

Optimization

The highest point of the ball occurs when vertical velocity equals zero, showing how calculus identifies critical moments.
